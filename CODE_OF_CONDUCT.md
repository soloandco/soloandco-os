# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or business status.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Protect private business and customer information.
- Accept correction and make room for less experienced contributors.
- Avoid sexualized language, harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Reports should be made privately through the Solo & Co GitHub organization contact channel. Do not disclose another person's private information in a public issue.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
